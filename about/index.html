Why Arquebus exists

After working across sales, business development and marketing at companies of different sizes and across various industries, one pattern stood out: businesses invested in paid and outbound acquisition while remaining difficult to discover during the research that shapes customer decisions.

Arquebus Intelligence brings brand, go-to-market (GTM) and search together to strengthen discovery, credibility and customer acquisition. We partner with ambitious founders and teams, combining commercial insight, data-driven strategy and hands-on execution to deliver an exceptional client experience.

How we think

  1. Start with the business, not the channel.We connect business objectives, commercial priorities and customer economics with sales, GTM, brand and search strategy. Every initiative should serve a wider acquisition goal.
  2. Build search around how buyers make decisions.We start with buyer research, understanding the questions, comparisons and information people need before choosing a business. Search visibility should reflect real customer behaviour and intent.
  3. Strengthen acquisition across every channel.Search should be a powerful acquisition channel in its own right, while strengthening paid advertising, outbound and other marketing efforts through greater brand visibility, credibility and buyer confidence. The goal is to improve overall acquisition performance, not simply replace one channel with another.
  4. Let data guide priorities and decisions.We use commercial performance, search insights and buyer behaviour to identify opportunities, prioritise execution and evaluate progress. Strategy should be grounded in evidence, not assumptions or vanity metrics.
  5. Obsess over the customer experience.From the first interaction to ongoing delivery, we value clarity, responsiveness, thoughtful execution and accountability. We aim to deliver an exceptional experience for our clients, supported by the same rigour and attention to detail we expect from our work.
