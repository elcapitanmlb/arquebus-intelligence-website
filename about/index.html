Why Arquebus exists

Manik Kamath founded Arquebus Intelligence after working across sales, business development and marketing at WeWork India and early-stage companies. One pattern stood out: businesses invested in paid and outbound acquisition while remaining difficult to discover during the research that shapes customer decisions.

Arquebus exists to address that gap by connecting buyer-led strategy with hands-on search and content implementation.

How we think

  • Start with the customer and their decision process.
  • Prioritise work by commercial relevance, not vanity metrics.
  • Use genuine expertise to create useful content.
  • Treat AI search as an evolving discovery channel, not a shortcut.
  • Measure honestly and avoid guarantees that search cannot support.
