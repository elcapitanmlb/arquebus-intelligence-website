How we assess the opportunity

Three perspectives. One commercial decision.

A strong opportunity sits where buyer demand, business value and achievable visibility overlap.

01

Customer economics

What is a new customer worth? Consider contract value, lifetime value, margin, retention, expansion potential and current acquisition costs.

  • Value of a qualified opportunity
  • Room to improve conversion or efficiency
  • Commercial goals the programme should support
02

Buying behaviour

How do customers reach a decision? Understand research depth, decision-makers, sales-cycle length, evaluation criteria and the questions or concerns that slow progress.

  • Questions buyers ask before contacting sales
  • Evidence and education needed to build trust
  • Where search can support the wider sales process
03

Search opportunity

Can your business credibly earn visibility for relevant questions and solutions? Assess buyer intent, demand themes, competitor visibility and content gaps.

  • High-intent topics and underserved questions
  • Competitor strengths and visibility gaps
  • Ability to create credible, useful content
+

The opportunity: focus where valuable customers are actively researching, your business has a credible answer, and improved visibility can support commercial outcomes.

A realistic time horizon

Built for sustained growth, not overnight results.

Search is an investment that develops over time. Technical improvements may show benefits sooner, while competitive visibility, content performance and measurable pipeline contribution can take longer.

We set priorities around your starting point, competition and commercial goals, then use performance data to refine the work. The aim is to build a stronger acquisition system that compounds, not to promise a fixed result by a fixed date.
