Who we help

Industry-agnostic. Commercially selective.

The best fit is defined by customer economics, buying behaviour and search opportunity — not an industry label alone.

What makes a business a good fit?

We look for established businesses where customers research, compare and need confidence before buying, and where a sustained search programme can make a meaningful commercial contribution.

  • Valuable customers, contracts or lifetime value
  • A research-intensive buying journey
  • A credible opportunity to capture or develop search demand
  • Capacity to invest USD 3,000–5,000+ per month
  • Access to a marketing owner and subject-matter experts
  • Willingness to measure progress over a sustained period

Initial prospecting priorities

Four sectors to explore first.

These are focus areas for prospect research, not exclusive categories or a guarantee of fit.

Financial services & insurance

Specialist insurance, fintech, complex financial products and advisory businesses where trust and comparison matter.

Cybersecurity & specialist technology

Cybersecurity, managed security, cloud infrastructure and specialist technology with technical evaluation journeys.

Healthcare & health technology

Specialist clinic groups, diagnostics and health technology businesses that need accurate, expert-reviewed information.

Education & certification

Professional certification, specialist training and established education providers with research-led enrolment decisions.

How we qualify opportunities

We assess ability to invest, customer economics, buyer research behaviour, acquisition pressure, search opportunity and implementation readiness. A large revenue figure or substantial ad budget alone does not make a business a good fit.

When we're probably not the right fit

  • You need guaranteed leads or immediate revenue within weeks.
  • You cannot sustain an ongoing programme long enough to learn and improve.
  • There is no credible search demand or educational content opportunity.
  • Your team cannot provide expertise, approvals or follow-up for new enquiries.
