What makes a strong fit

Four signals that search can create meaningful value.

We look beyond industry labels. The strongest fit comes from how customers buy, what a customer is worth, and whether there is a credible opportunity to earn visibility.

01Buying behaviour⌕

Research-intensive buyer journeys

Your customers explore problems, compare solutions, assess risk and revisit questions before they speak to sales. Journeys may span weeks or months and involve multiple stakeholders. Search Infrastructure helps you answer important questions and build familiarity throughout that process.

Longer sales cycles, considered decisions and repeated buyer research.
02Credibility◇

Expertise, education and trust matter

Your offering needs explanation, your expertise sets you apart, or buyers need evidence before they believe your claims. Clear positioning, expert perspectives and useful educational content make your value easier to discover, understand and trust.

Complex offers, meaningful trust barriers and expertise worth sharing.
03Customer economics↗

Acquisition has to make commercial sense

Customer value, contract size, margins, retention or expansion potential should justify sustained acquisition investment. Improving lead quality, conversion or acquisition efficiency should have a meaningful effect on business performance.

Healthy customer economics and a clear reason to improve acquisition.
04Growth mindset⟳

A commitment to compounding growth

Search visibility, topical authority and buyer confidence take time to build. We work best with teams prepared to prioritise consistently, share subject-matter expertise, learn from performance and improve the programme over time.

Consistent ownership, access to expertise and realistic expectations.

How we assess the opportunity

Three perspectives. One commercial decision.

A strong opportunity sits where buyer demand, business value and achievable visibility overlap.

01

Customer economics

What is a new customer worth? Consider contract value, lifetime value, margin, retention, expansion potential and current acquisition costs.

  • Value of a qualified opportunity
  • Room to improve conversion or efficiency
  • Commercial goals the programme should support
02

Buying behaviour

How do customers reach a decision? Understand research depth, decision-makers, sales-cycle length, evaluation criteria and the questions or concerns that slow progress.

  • Questions buyers ask before contacting sales
  • Evidence and education needed to build trust
  • Where search can support the wider sales process
03

Search opportunity

Can your business credibly earn visibility for relevant questions and solutions? Assess buyer intent, demand themes, competitor visibility and content gaps.

  • High-intent topics and underserved questions
  • Competitor strengths and visibility gaps
  • Ability to create credible, useful content
+

The opportunity: focus where valuable customers are actively researching, your business has a credible answer, and improved visibility can support commercial outcomes.

A realistic time horizon

Built for sustained growth, not overnight results.

Search is an investment that develops over time. Technical improvements may show benefits sooner, while competitive visibility, content performance and measurable pipeline contribution can take longer.

We set priorities around your starting point, competition and commercial goals, then use performance data to refine the work. The aim is to build a stronger acquisition system that compounds, not to promise a fixed result by a fixed date.
